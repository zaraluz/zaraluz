About me

I’m a results-driven Data Analyst specialized in designing and implementing end-to-end data environments, structuring analytical architectures from operational systems to cloud-based data warehouses.

I have proven experience in building scalable ETL/ELT pipelines, automating data workflows, and delivering KPI-driven dashboards that improve business visibility and support strategic decision-making.
